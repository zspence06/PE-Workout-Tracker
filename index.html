❓ How to use the tracker

Two minutes to read. You only have to do steps 1 and 2 once all term.

1
Log in

Your name + a 4-digit PIN you make up. Use the same two every single time, on any phone or Chromebook.

2
Build your routine — once

Pick your exercises on the Build My Routine tab. You need two workouts:
Workout A = 4 lower body + 2 core.
Workout B = 4 upper body + 2 core.

3
Every class after that

Pick Workout A or Workout B at the top. Open Do My Workout. Tick each exercise as you finish it. Hit Submit before you leave.

The one rule that matters: ticking a box is not the same as submitting. Your teacher only sees the workout once you press ✅ Submit Workout.

Questions people actually ask

How do I know whether today is A or B?

The tracker picks for you. It looks at the last workout you actually submitted and offers the other one — that alternating is the whole point of an A/B program. It tells you why, right under the buttons: “Last workout: A on 9/18 — so today is probably Workout B.”

It is only a suggestion. If Mr. Spencer says otherwise, just tap the other button.

What does “4 of 6” on the green bar mean?

Six is the daily requirement: 4 from your main area (lower body on A, upper body on B) + 2 core. The bar counts composed exercises, not just any six.

So six core exercises scores 2 of 6, not 6 — core is capped at 2 because that is what the program asks for. Doing extra is great and it still gets logged; it just does not push the bar past 6.

I pressed Submit and it said no. Why?

Look at the red ✗ list right above the Submit button — it shows everything in your way at once, not one thing at a time. The usual three:

  • Not enough ticked. You need 4 + 2 checked off.
  • Already in your history. You logged that exercise earlier today on the Quick Log tab. Press Skip it and submit the rest — it already counts.
  • Workout is empty. Nothing added to that day yet. Go build it.
What is the difference between “Do My Workout” and “Quick Log”?

Do My Workout is your normal day: your saved A/B routine with tick boxes and a Submit button. Use this one.

Quick Log is for a one-off that is not part of your routine — a fitness test, a make-up day, something the sub had you do. Type it in and press Commit.

It says some of my exercises are “0 lbs”.

That is a heads-up, not a refusal — you can still submit. It is correct for push-ups, planks and anything bodyweight.

But if you actually loaded a bar, type the weight into the Lbs box before you submit. A lift saved as 0 lbs wrecks your Last Time number, and beating Last Time is the entire point.

What is the “Last Time” column for?

It is the sets, reps and weight you did the last time you performed that exact exercise. That is the number to beat. Adding one rep or 5 lbs to last time is the whole point of the program — that is progressive overload, and it is what makes you stronger.

I forgot my PIN.

Nobody can look it up — it is not stored anywhere, which is what stops other students getting into your account. Ask Mr. Spencer to Reset PIN for you. Your workouts all come across to the new PIN; you lose nothing.

Do not just make a new account under a slightly different name. You will lose your history.

The date at the top is wrong.

It should say today. If it does not, you will see an orange warning — tap the date box and set it back to today, or your workout gets filed under the wrong day and may not be graded.

It is meant to be changed only when you are catching up a day you genuinely missed.

I picked the wrong class period.

Ask Mr. Spencer — he can move you from his dashboard in one tap. If you move yourself with a class link, he gets told, so just ask.

Can I do both Workout A and Workout B on the same day?

Yes — a make-up day, or a double session. Submit one, switch with the Workout A / Workout B buttons at the top, and submit the other. Each is tracked separately, and ↩️ Undo Submit only ever undoes the workout it sits under.

The workout that is not the one you picked is dimmed with a “tap to switch” bar, so you cannot submit the wrong one by accident.

I submitted by accident.

↩️ Undo Submit under the same workout, same day. It pulls those rows back out of your history so you can fix them and submit again.

Am I allowed to add an exercise that is not on the list?

Yes. On Build My Routine, choose your muscle group, then pick + Add Custom Exercise… at the bottom of the exercise list and type the name. Check with Mr. Spencer that it counts for the area you are putting it under.

Can I get my plan on paper?

Yes — 🖨 Print Workout Plan on the Do My Workout tab prints both workouts with blank boxes for sets, reps and weight, plus your Last Time numbers to beat. Good for days the wifi is down.

Is my phone saving this, or is it on the internet?

The internet — so you can log in on any device and everything is there. Watch the bottom of the screen: ✅ Saved means it went through. If you ever see a red NOT SAVED box, keep the tab open and check your wifi; do not close it.

❓ Running the tracker

Everything you need is on this page. The Firebase console is only ever needed to publish security rules — not for day-to-day teaching.

1
Set up once, at the start of a term

On Class Periods, add a class for each hour you teach. Put them in your real timetable order with the ↑↓ buttons — that order drives the student picker and the grouping on this page.

Then Copy link for each class and paste it into that period's Google Classroom. A student who opens it is placed in that class automatically.

2
Start of a period

Set the day's focus on Learning Target. Students see it in the banner and as a pop-up the first time they open the tracker that hour.

3
End of a period — the 10-second check

On Today & Roster, tap Not started. Whoever is left on screen has not logged anything today. That is your whole attendance-for-effort check.

Grading note: the Exercises Logged number counts individual exercise rows, not sessions. A student who does a full six-exercise workout adds 6, not 1. To count sessions, use Print report and count the dates.

Reading the roster

Do the big tiles at the top follow the class filter?

Yes. Pick 3rd Hour and every tile counts only 3rd Hour — the line above them says which class you are looking at and how many students that is.

They deliberately do not follow the status chips or the search box. Those are a drill-down inside the class you already chose; a tile that emptied itself as you tapped Not started would not tell you anything.

Does this page update on its own while I watch it?

Nothing polls on a timer — that would cost tens of thousands of database reads a day to tell you something you can ask for. Instead the page is loud about its own age: the stamp beside the tiles turns amber after two minutes and red after ten (“This is 12 minutes old — press Refresh”).

It also refreshes itself when you switch back to this tab. Before an end-of-period call, glance at the stamp; if it is not green, press ↻ Refresh.

What does “✓ Recount totals” do, and when should I use it?

It reads every workout of every student in the class you have selected and corrects their term totals against the real rows. It then tells you exactly what it changed — e.g. “Corrected 2 term totals: Alex Smith 48 → 9” — or says everything already matched.

Use it before you grade off the term totals, not as a refresh. It is the expensive operation on this page, which is why it is a button rather than something that runs on its own.

Opening a student's row and printing a report also correct every total they touch, and say so the same way.

What do the green bars next to each name mean?

Progress against today's requirement, scored out of 6: 4 from the day's main area (lower body on A, upper body on B) + 2 core. It turns solid green at 6 of 6.

“+2” after the score means two extra exercises beyond the requirement — they get logged and graded, they just do not push the bar past 6.

A bar only ever describes today. Yesterday's finished workout reads as Not started today, not as a full bar.

Does the tracker make students alternate A and B?

It suggests it. On a new day the toggle pre-selects the opposite of the last workout that student actually submitted, and shows them why. They can override it in one tap, so a student doing two Workout As in a row is a choice, not a trap — but you will see far fewer of them than you used to.

One-off Quick Log entries are ignored by that calculation; only real A/B submissions count as "the last workout".

What is the difference between “6/6” and “submitted”?

6 of 6 means they ticked everything off. Submitted means they pressed the button that actually sends it to you.

A student sitting at 6/6 and not submitted did the work and forgot the last step. Worth a shout across the gym before the bell — use the In progress chip.

Somebody has a red MOVED THEMSELVES tag.

They used another period's class link and moved themselves in the last seven days. Usually innocent — a friend sent them a link. If they are in the wrong period, fix it with the dropdown on their row; the tag clears itself after a week.

Students are showing up under a red “Unassigned” heading.

They registered without picking a class — normally because they were mid-registration when a class was archived. Use the dropdown on their row to put them in the right period.

The jobs you will actually do

A student forgot their PIN.

Press Reset PIN on their row, type a new 4-digit PIN, and tell them. Their entire workout history comes with them — nothing is lost.

Nobody, including you, can look up an existing PIN. That is deliberate: it is what stops one student opening another's account. Resetting is the only route, and it is fine.

Two students have the same name.

The second one will be told the name is taken and asked to add a middle initial or period number, e.g. “Alex Smith B”. If one of them registered wrongly, delete the empty account and have them register again.

How much should I trust the “Exercises Logged (all term)” tile?

Treat it as a running estimate, not a gradebook figure. It adds up a counter stored on each student record so the dashboard can show a whole class without downloading everyone's workouts. That counter is rebuilt from the real rows whenever that student next logs in, so it self-corrects — but a student who has not opened the app in a fortnight can be carrying a stale number.

Anywhere it actually matters, the app reads the real rows instead — and when it does, it corrects the stored number. Opening a student's row and running a Print report both fix every count they touch, so the roster gets truer the more you use it. Delete counts for real before it asks you, and says so if the dashboard had been showing something different.

For a defensible grade, print the report.

A student has two accounts with real work in both.

There is no merge. Deleting either one deletes its workouts with it.

Print both records first (open each row, or print the class), decide which account they keep, reconcile the missing sessions by hand, then delete the other. Rare, but worth doing in that order — the delete is irreversible.

Printing for grading.

Pick a class in the dropdown (or leave it on All classes), choose a date range, and press Print report. You get every workout for every student in that range, one class per page, ready for a gradebook.

The report follows whatever you have on screen — the class dropdown, the status chips and the search box — and prints the filter it used at the top of every page. Filter to Not started and print, and you get exactly that list.

Narrow the date range if it warns you about the row count — “last 7 days” is usually what you want for a weekly effort grade.

Why can I archive a class but not delete one?

Deliberate. Deleting a class would orphan every workout logged under it. Archive kills its invite link and hides it from the student picker; students already in it keep working and you keep all their data.

Do not archive every class at once — students cannot use the tracker without at least one active class to join.

Deleting a student.

Permanent, double-confirmed, and it removes every workout they logged plus their claim on their name. Use it for test accounts and duplicate registrations, not for students who have left — for those, just leave the record; it costs nothing.

A student swears they submitted and I cannot see it.

Three things to check, in order:

  • The date on their screen. If it is not today, they filed it under another day. Open their row — the workout will be there under that date.
  • Did they press Submit, or only tick the boxes? Ticks alone are not a submission.
  • Which account? On a shared device, a mistyped name creates a separate record. Search their surname here — a near-duplicate will show up.
Change your teacher password.

🔑 Change Teacher Password, top right. This one password is what protects every student's data on this dashboard, so it should not be a school-mascot word.

If it refuses, sign out of the dashboard and back in first — it requires a recent login.

Something is not loading.

Almost always the network. The page tells you when a save fails rather than failing quietly, so if you have no red banner, the data is there. Reload once before assuming anything is wrong.
